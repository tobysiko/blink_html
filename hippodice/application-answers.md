## The one thing to settle first

**Playing time.** The form's longest bucket is 61–90 minutes and the terms measure "a regular
game with the recommended number of players, without rules explanation or setup". The rulebook,
the site and the one-pager currently say **60–120**. Three options, in order of preference:

1. Time two or three real games at 3–4 players and restate the range honestly. This is the only
   option that also fixes the rulebook.
2. State 60–90 and cap it in the rules — the market-thinning end trigger already bounds the
   length, so this may simply be true.
3. Don't enter this year.

Entering with 61–90 selected while the rulebook says 120 is the one combination to avoid: a
juror who opens both sees the contradiction, and the prototype round measures it anyway.
