WHY REDISTRIBUTION ONLY GOES SO FAR
-----------------------------------
At the moment of choosing a meld, what caps its size?
    the BAND limit binds    35% of the time
    the HAND binds          65% of the time

Best meld the hand can legally play, by cards remaining (a hand starts at 10
and is played down to zero before it recycles):

    cards in hand   share of decisions   mean best meld
        10                21.8%               3.14
         7                 8.5%               2.76
         5                 7.0%               2.29
         3                10.0%               1.65
         2                12.0%               1.14
         1                16.5%               1.00

Almost 40% of all meld decisions are taken with three or fewer cards in hand,
where no large meld is possible whatever the band allows. The ladder's top is
gated by hand depletion, not by the reserve.
