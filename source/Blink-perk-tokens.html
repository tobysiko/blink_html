OPTIONAL MODULE

Perk tokens

The full rules are in the perks module document. In short: shuffle the 19 tokens and deal four to each player. Lay one above each of slots 1 to 4 of your victory row, face up — your choice which goes where, and it is permanent once a card reaches the row. Slot 5 stays empty.
At each recycle, after income and before you pick up your discard, turn every token face up and arm one perk whose slot your row reaches: slot 4 at two cards, slot 3 at three, slot 2 at four and slot 1 at all five. Turn the armed token sideways. It runs until your next recycle, even if you spend the card that unlocked it. SPEND perks turn face down when used; STANDING perks never turn over.
Cutting: the dashed rectangles are cuts. The dotted line down the middle of each token, marked with a triangle at each end, is a fold — fold it backwards so both printed faces end up outside, and glue.
