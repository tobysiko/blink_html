PROPOSAL · UNTESTED · NOT PART OF THE GAME

Victory row perks

Shuffle the tokens and deal four to each player. Put one on each of slots 1 to 4 of your victory row, face up — your choice which goes where, and it is permanent once a card reaches the row. The fifth slot stays blank: it fills on your first research, so a perk there would be a baseline everyone has rather than a bet anyone made.
A perk is live while its slot holds a card. Cards fill the row from the right, so slot 4 wakes at two cards, slot 3 at three, slot 2 at four and slot 1 needs all five — the slot is the bet. Spend a perk by turning its token over; turn every token back when your hand recycles.
The decks are flavour only: every perk is equal, and any perk may go on any slot.
Cutting: the dashed rectangles are cuts. The dotted line down the middle of each token, marked with a triangle at each end, is a fold — fold it backwards so both printed faces end up outside, and glue.
