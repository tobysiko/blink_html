The cost of matching the winner — and how often you cashed

Match the winner's card count and lose, and one card you played is set aside for 1 gold and goes to the shared pile; the meld that ranked last takes 1 gold. Tally one mark per card you cashed instead of using on the map (the sim predicts about one in three). Count for yourself only.

cards cashed ______ of ______ played  ·  did cashing ever feel like the obvious choice, with no decision in it? yes / no
