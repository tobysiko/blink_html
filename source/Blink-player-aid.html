Round. Melds face down · reveal · declare A, leader first · highest total wins · winner's die = meld size · spend in that order. Matched the winner's count and lost: set one aside, +1 gold. Last: +1 gold.

Recycle. Hand empty, meld spent — at once, mid-turn: 1 gold per objective you hold · pick your discard up as your ten. Nothing is drawn.

Meld. Any unbroken run; duplicates free, suits irrelevant. 2-3-3-4-4 yes · 2-2-4-4 no (no 3).

Duel. Your card vs theirs + ground; a level fight is yours unless theirs matches the ground.

End. Reserve empty at round's end: one more round. Score units · row + centre · objectives.
