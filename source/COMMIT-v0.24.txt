v0.24: combat you can see, and a purse that explains itself

COMBAT
- the card you SPEND is the attack. The attacker is never asked for a second
  card from hand, so which card you send is the whole decision. Before this the
  rank of the spent card did nothing at all: you could storm a Mountain with a
  2 as long as you held a 19 back.
- a fortification is a price, not a veto. One card cannot attack it; two can,
  and the LOWER of the two ranks fights. "Two of the same suit" was measured
  out first - a meld holds two of one suit only 9-14% of the time, which is a
  prohibition wearing a price tag.
- a won duel that empties the tile takes the ground. Measured: a bot forbidden
  to attack used to beat one that attacked 56/44 under the OLD gold rule, so
  combat was already a trap; this is the only change tested that flips it
  (51/49). See DUEL-SPOILS.md.
- declining an assault used to eat the card that declared it. It now cashes.
- the contested tile is marked on the map for as long as the question stands,
  and the attacking card is shown beside the prompt with the rank needed to
  hold it. zone_test fails if either is missing.

GOLD
- exploring with a card of rank 10 or under pays 1. Rank 10 is the top of every
  starting deck and the bottom of the market, so the tap is open while you are
  poor and closes as you upgrade. +4.2 gold a game, walls 2.9 -> 3.5, and the
  upgrade race untouched at 34.4. "always" and "seams" stay selectable so the
  comparison that chose this can be re-run.
- Game.purse() is now the only way a purse changes. Sixteen declared reasons,
  each writing a caption where the coin lands AND a sentence in the log, in
  both languages. purse_test forbids raw mutation, refuses an undeclared or
  dead reason, and requires the log to agree with the counters to the coin -
  which immediately caught gold_in_cashed being double-counted on every bot
  cash.

FIXES

  pasted into the en block, where duplicate keys silently win. i18n_test now
  reads the source, which is the only place the evidence survives.
- check_rules.py and check_figs.py crashed when run from outside source/.
- build_pdfs.sh failed at the last step in a folder that forbids deletes.
- new check_fonts.py: the PDFs have NEVER embedded Fraunces or IBM Plex Sans,
  because every builder fetches them from Google at render time and falls back
  silently. Install them locally and the whole toolchain works offline.
