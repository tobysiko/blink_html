An optional module for the base rules v0.26

Perks

A job for every slot of your victory row

01

What the module adds

Perks give the slots of your victory row a job of their own. Every rule of the base game still applies; this module adds one step to the recycle and a small hand of tokens beside your board.

Exactly one perk runs at a time, and you choose which at every recycle, from the perks your row is deep enough to reach at that moment. A deep row does not run four perks — it chooses from four. No perk runs before your first recycle.

Once armed, a perk keeps running until your next recycle, even if you spend the victory card that unlocked it. Spending a victory card costs you nothing now; it shows up at the next recycle, as a shorter menu.

02

Components

  • 19 perk tokens — one of each perk listed in §05. Each is a fold-over token: a ready face and a spent face. Print them from the perk token sheet, cut along the dashed lines and fold along the dotted one.
03

Setup

Do this after the base game's setup, before the first card is played.

  1. Shuffle the perk tokens and deal four to each player. Return the rest to the box unseen.
  2. Lay one token on the table just above each of slots 1, 2, 3 and 4 of your victory row, ready face up, in any order you like. Slot 5 stays empty: it fills on your first research, so a perk there would be a gift.
  3. You may rearrange your tokens until your first card reaches your victory row. From then on the arrangement is permanent.

Which slot

The victory row fills from the right, so the slot decides how long you wait before a perk can be armed:

SlotReachable when your row holdsThe bet
42 cardsWorking almost at once. The safe place for the perk you want most.
33 cardsReachable in most games.
24 cardsLate, and only if you keep researching.
1all 5A long shot: fill the row and never spend it down.

Every perk is equal in strength; what differs is how soon you want each one, and how much of your row you will tie up to reach it.

04

Playing with perks

At every recycle

The module adds one step to the recycle (base rules §08), after income and before you pick up your discard:

  1. Collect your income, as in the base game.
  2. Arm a perk. Turn all your perk tokens ready face up. Then choose one of the perks whose slot your row reaches right now, and turn that token sideways to show it is armed. Any perk you had armed before stops. If your row reaches none, or you would rather have none, nothing is armed.
  3. Pick up your whole discard as your new hand, as in the base game.

Only the armed perk works. The others wait, face up, for a later recycle.

The two kinds

  • SPEND — use it once, then turn the token face down. It turns ready face up again at your next recycle.
  • STANDING — it simply works for as long as it is armed. These tokens never turn over.

Each token says which kind it is.

The price of a perk

Every victory card you spend on A, B or C is a slot your row may no longer reach at the next recycle. The more you value your perks, the fewer effects you will spend — a player who wants every slot in reach has quietly given up their war chest.
